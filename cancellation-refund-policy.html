Cancellation & Refund Policy

Effective date: 28 September 2026

1. How to cancel

To cancel a booking, please call our customer support team on +91 9702988465 and provide your Booking ID and a valid reason for cancellation. You may also email carwithdriver.vikhroli@gmail.com.

2. Cancellation and refund windows

Time before pickupPolicy
24 hours or moreFull refund of the eligible amount paid.
3 to under 24 hoursNo cancellation charge. Refunds may be reduced only by non-refundable payment-gateway or transaction-processing charges, if any, that cannot be recovered by us.
Within 3 hours100% cancellation charge. No refund.

3. Refund processing

Eligible refunds are normally processed by us within 3 working days after approval. Bank, card, UPI or payment-provider settlement times may take additional time after we initiate the refund.

4. Refund method

Where possible, refunds are sent to the original payment method used for the booking, such as UPI, bank account or card. If the original method cannot accept a refund, our team may contact you to arrange another appropriate method.

5. Requesting or following up on a refund

Please provide your Booking ID when contacting us. For a delayed refund, call +91 9702988465 or email carwithdriver.vikhroli@gmail.com.
