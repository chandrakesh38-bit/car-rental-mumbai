Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our self-drive and with-driver car rentals[cite: 2].

Who can rent a car from us?

Anyone who is 21 years or older can rent a car. You must have a valid Indian LMV (Light Motor Vehicle) driving licence that is at least 6 months old. You must show the original driving licence or a valid DigiLocker version at the start of the trip[cite: 2].

Can international tourists, foreigners, or NRIs rent a car?

Yes. International travellers and NRIs can book a self-drive rental car in India. You need: (1) A valid International Driving Licence, (2) Your Passport, and (3) An International Driving Permit (IDP) that is valid in India[cite: 2].

How does the car booking process work?

Booking a self-drive or chauffeur car is simple: Open the website → choose your service → select pickup date and time → choose your car model → send request on WhatsApp → Our team will verify and confirm[cite: 2].

How early should I book my rental car?

You can book as late as 3 hours before your trip start time. However, for better car availability and lower prices, we recommend booking 1–2 days in advance, especially on weekends and holidays[cite: 2].

What is the minimum and maximum rental duration?

Rental Plans: Minimum 8 hours, maximum 60 days.
Subscription Plans: Minimum 7 days, up to 24 months[cite: 2].

Are pets allowed in the rental car?

No. Pets are strictly not allowed because some customers may have allergies. If found, a penalty will be charged[cite: 2].
