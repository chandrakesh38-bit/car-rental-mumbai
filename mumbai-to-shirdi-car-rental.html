Mumbai to Shirdi Pilgrimage Overview

Traveling to Shirdi from Mumbai is now fast and smooth via the Samruddhi Mahamarg (Hindu Hrudaysamrat Balasaheb Thackeray Maharashtra Samruddhi Mahamarg). The round-trip covers roughly 480 KM and offers comfortable highway connectivity.

Trip Options: Round-trip service is available, and one-way requests are also accepted. For any one-way outstation booking, billing is calculated on a round-trip basis because the vehicle returns to its operating base after the customer drop.
