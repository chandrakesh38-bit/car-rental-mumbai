Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 October 2026

1. Information we collect

We may collect information you provide when you request or manage a booking, including your name, mobile number, email address, pickup or delivery location, trip details and booking reference. For self-drive bookings, we may also request identity and eligibility documents such as Aadhaar, Driving Licence, PAN and current address proof for manual verification.

2. How we use information

We use information to process booking enquiries, verify availability and eligibility, communicate with you, arrange vehicles and drivers, process payments, provide support, prevent misuse, maintain records and improve our website and services.

3. Analytics and advertising

Our website uses Google Analytics and may use Google Ads measurement to understand website usage and advertising performance. We do not intentionally send your name, email address, phone number or identity-document contents to Google Analytics as event parameters.

Optional session analytics

With your permission, we use Microsoft Clarity to understand website interactions through session recordings and heatmaps and improve the booking experience. It may collect clicks, scrolling, device and browser information, page visits and non-identifying booking-stage events, using cookies and similar technologies. We do not intentionally send your name, phone number, email address, OTP, payment credentials or booking identifier as custom events or tags. Form inputs and dropdowns are masked by Clarity, and we additionally mask customer-specific location and booking-confirmation content. Administrative, invoice, quotation and document-verification pages are excluded.

You can decline optional session analytics or change your choice later using Analytics preferences in the website footer. Declining does not prevent booking. Microsoft processes Clarity data under the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

4. Service providers

We may use service providers that help us operate the service, including Google for maps, analytics and advertising, MSG91 or related verification services for OTP, Razorpay for payment links and payment processing, Supabase for application data and secure storage, and hosting or email providers. These providers process information according to their own terms and privacy obligations.

5. Payments

Payments may be made through secure Razorpay links or other approved payment methods. Card, UPI and banking credentials are handled by the relevant payment provider; we do not ask customers to send card PINs, UPI PINs or OTPs to us.

6. Self-drive documents

Self-drive documents are used for manual verification and are intended to be accessible only to authorized reviewers. Please do not share secure document-upload links. We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose collected, business records, dispute handling, fraud prevention and applicable legal or regulatory obligations.

7. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to ask about the personal information associated with your booking or to request correction or deletion where applicable, subject to records we must retain for legal, accounting, fraud-prevention or dispute purposes.
